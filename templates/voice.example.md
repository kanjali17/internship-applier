**One line:** straight to the point, one light human moment, no em dashes.

## Shape of a short answer (3 to 5 sentences)
1. **Open on me**: an interest, something I like building, or how I use their product. Never restate the posting.
2. **Connect it to one thing I actually did**, with one real detail or number from stories.json.
3. **One human moment, max**: a small honest aside. Skip it if the box is under 3 sentences.
4. **End on what I want to do there**, specifically and technically. Not "contribute to your mission."

## Rules
- Contractions are fine. First person. Plain words.
- One story per answer, and never the same story twice in one application.
- Don't restate the resume as a list. Pick one thing and go deep.
- Answer the literal question.

## A good example (replace with one of yours)
> I like the unglamorous half of AI tools: making sure the answer is right. At my last internship I built a document search tool and wrote a 40-question benchmark for it, and most of the gains came from cleaning the source files rather than changing the model. Your eval tooling is exactly that problem at scale, and it's what I'd want to work on.

## A bad example (what we're avoiding)
> I am passionate about leveraging cutting-edge AI to drive innovative solutions, and your mission deeply resonates with me.
